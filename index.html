Privacy Policy for Color Dhamal

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Color Dhamal is a casual color-sorting puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use the game.

Advertising

Color Dhamal uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Rewarded Ads

Players may voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to receive in-game rewards such as coins. Rewards have no real-world monetary value.

Game Data

The game may store gameplay information such as coin balance, level progress, daily rewards, settings, and other game progress locally on the player's device.

Third-Party Services

Color Dhamal may use Google services including Google AdMob and Google Play services. These services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by the game may process information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the game. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game or its services change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Color Dhamal Google Play Store listing.
